# ASL demo animation references

These are original procedural animation keyframes for the existing Signify rig. They approximate source-described signs and have not been independently validated by an ASL signer. They are not a teaching resource or general sentence translation. No reference images or videos are redistributed.

- **Hi**: Side-to-side greeting wave, palm outward. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/h/hello.htm
- **Thank you**: Flat dominant hand begins near lips and moves forward and slightly down. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/t/thankyou.htm
- **I love you**: ILY handshape: thumb, physical index and little finger extended; middle and ring folded. A lexicalized ILY sign, not sequential fingerspelling. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/i/ily.htm
- **Yes**: S handshape makes two wrist nods. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/y/yes.htm
- **No**: Physical index and middle fingers close to the thumb twice, with a small negative headshake. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/n/no.htm
- **Hello**: Flat B hand, fingertips touch forehead then sweep outward and to the side — the standard ASL salute-style hello. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/h/hello.htm
- **Good morning**: Compound GOOD + MORNING, with a supporting base palm/forearm. See also g/good.htm. Contact is approximate on this stylized rig. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/m/morning.htm
- **Bye**: Relaxed open-hand farewell wave, palm outward, hand oscillates side to side — the universal goodbye gesture used in ASL. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/b/bye.htm
- **Please**: Flat hand circles on the chest. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/p/please.htm
- **Sorry**: A hand circles on the chest; apologetic head inclination. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/s/sorry.htm
- **Help**: The base palm lifts the dominant loose-thumb fist. Neutral HELP, without directional inflection. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/h/help.htm
- **Welcome**: Invitation sense: flat palm draws inward. Not a response to thank you. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/w/welcome.htm
- **Name**: Two H hands cross and tap twice. The word NAME, not a person's name. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/n/name.htm
- **Family**: Two F hands trace an enclosing circle. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/f/family.htm
- **Food**: Gathered fingertips approach the mouth twice (citation form). Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/f/food.htm
- **Eat**: Gathered fingertips approach the mouth once. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/e/eat.htm
- **Water**: W handshape approaches the mouth twice. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/w/water.htm
- **School**: Dominant flat palm taps the upward base palm twice. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/s/school.htm
- **Work**: Employment/labor sense: dominant S fist taps the base fist/wrist twice. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/w/work.htm
- **Mother**: Open 5 hand, thumb near the chin. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/m/mom.htm
- **Father**: Open 5 hand, thumb near the forehead. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/d/dad.htm
- **Where**: Extended index, wrist pivots side to side; furrowed brows. Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/w/where.htm
- **What**: Relaxed open palms move outward with furrowed brows (general question form). Reference: https://www.lifeprint.com/asl101/pages-signs/w/what.htm

All clips have their own motion, handshape, entry, hold, and exit poses. Both arms have full joint tracks. The right-hand index/thumb mapping is repaired. Fingers have independent MCP/PIP/DIP controls, thumbs have three controls, and forearm roll is separated from bounded wrist bending. Meshes use rigid segments, not skin deformation; contact, collision avoidance and facial grammar remain approximate. All assets await independent ASL review.
